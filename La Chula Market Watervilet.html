About

A Mexican market and restaurant, right on Main Street

La Chula is a neighborhood spot in downtown Watervliet where you can sit down, grab food to go, or have it delivered. Guests mention the guacamole, the horchata and the friendly people, and plenty of them take something home on the way out.
